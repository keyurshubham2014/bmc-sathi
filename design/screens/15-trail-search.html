What a trail shows

5 stages

One record followed from the first complaint to what happened after the repair.

  1. 01Complaints

    What citizens reported, in their own words, with upvotes.

  2. 02Issue

    Complaints about the same place, joined into one issue.

  3. 03Work order

    The job that was assigned: contractor, method, cost and dates.

  4. 04Road

    The road and segment, its surface and when it was last resurfaced.

  5. 05Outcome

    Whether the repair held or re-opened within 30 days, and the SLA.

A stage with nothing recorded stays in the trail with an empty marker, for example No complaint linked.
